⚔️ Pet Battles

A RuneLite plugin that turns your Old School RuneScape collection-log pets into a turn-based battle team. Collect pets in-game, level them with your gameplay, and challenge themed AI trainers across Gielinor.

Type effectiveness

Ten OSRS-flavoured types built on the classic combat triangle — Melee > Ranged > Magic > Melee — extended with Fire, Ice, Nature, Undead, Demon, Dragon, and Skilling. Read a row as the attacker: green cells are the types it hits for double damage, red cells are the types that resist it.

Trainers

Every trainer runs a themed party of real OSRS pets. Beat the story ladder from Party Pete to the Wise Old Man, catch random-event challengers when they appear, or hunt down all 23 skillcape masters for the toughest matchups.
